## Definitions

> [!tip] IDENTITY
> (Identity of a person) « The characteristics they have that distinguish them from others. »

> [!tip] NATIONAL
> « Relates to the whole of a country or nation, rather to than part of it or to other nations »

> [!question] NATIONAL IDENTITY
 « The public image of an imagined community, projecting an illusion of unity reflected symbolically in a flag, a national anthem, and distinctive rituals, culturally represented [...] and transmitted by social institions, in particular the educational system and the mass media. »
